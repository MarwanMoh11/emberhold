# 02 · Regions

One entry per region: what it is for, what is in it, and what it asks of the player. Coordinates are not repeated here. For the exact contents of a region, run:

```bash
node docs/world/tools/render.mjs --region <id>
```

## Emberhold `hold` · tier 0

**Layout.** The Ember Rise, above the Old King's Bridge. The palisade is today's rampart ring, the same 1280 × 1120 shape with four gates. Inside it, today's pads keep their relative places: the hall at the top of the rise, the depot on the south road just below it, the muster yards (barracks, archery range) facing the South Gate and the bridge.

**The bridgehead.** A horseshoe of wall (hall 2) wraps the bridge's north landing, both ends on the bank, with two towers behind it. Holding the bridgehead means the south approach never reaches the palisade. This is the first fortification the game teaches, and the reason the hold sits where it does.

**Around it.** There is a small copse and rocks inside the ring, so the first minute has no walk.

## The Barrow Downs `downs` · tier 1

**Resources.** Open loam north of the hold, the best north-bank farmland (two farms flank the King's Road) and the Barrow Copse for timber.

**Points of interest.** The **Barrow King's** mound in the north is being torn open by the **Barrow Diggers**, the nearest camp and the first one most players burn. Breaking the mound gives the Barrow Crown relic. Three barrows, a tithe barn and the standing stones fill the rest.

**Outpost.** **Kingsbarrow Cross** sits where the King's Road meets the Highroad. It is the hinge for Frostmere.

**Night.** The north raid comes down the King's Road to the North Gate until the Diggers burn.

## Whisperwood `whisperwood` · tier 1

**Resources.** Deep timber in rows, as if someone planted it. Two lumber clearings: one at the forest edge nearest the hold, and a deeper one with the heaviest timber in the near country.

**Camp.** **Thornstake Camp** sits deep in the wood on the old track west and feeds the north-west raid.

**Outpost.** **Woodcutters' Rest**, on the Wood Road.

**Connections.** The region leads on to the optional Deepwood.

## Hollow Village `hollow` · tier 1

**What it is.** Survivors hide in the cellar under the green, and **Rotwood Camp** in the rotten wood above the village is why. Burn Rotwood and they come up: +6 population and two villagers.

**Buildings.**
- Rebuilt houses (three pads).
- A river meadow farm.
- A fishery on the Emberflow.
- **Millford**, the west approach's ford. Its barricade (hall 3) and two towers sit on the north bank.

**Teaches.** The survivors mechanic, and that a ford is slow: enemies wade at 0.6× speed under arrow fire.

## Greyfall Scarp `greyfall` · tier 2

**Resources.** Quarries at the foot of the Greyfall Escarpment. The Shrine of the Mason (+20% wall and gate health) sits under the loose face.

**Crossings.**
- The **Scarp Stair** switchbacks up to Frostmere.
- The **Gorge Bridge** is the east approach, and the only bridge over the gorge. The **Gorge Gate** is a short horseshoe at its west landing (hall 3) and holds the whole flank cheaply.

**Camp.** The **Scarp Warcamp**'s slingers sit on the ledge over the gorge road.

**Outpost.** **Scarpfoot**.

## The Ferrow Fields `ferrow` · tier 2

**What it is.** The best soil on the frontier, over the bridge: big floodplain farms and the Shrine of the Harvest. **Ferrow Cross** (the outpost) is where the south road, the lane west and the road east meet.

**The muster.** The **Ferrow Muster** is where the south approach gathers, and the first night comes from it. The mill families won't move while it stands.

**Burning it.**
- The south approach falls back to the Stairwarden, on the Rim.
- The Rim becomes claimable.
- The farms stop being the front line.

**Teaches.** Burning a muster pushes the front back. This is the game's central strategic idea.

## Frostmere `frostmere` · tier 3

**What it is.** A cold lake under the peaks, with high pinewood to the west and far-shore timber to the east. Frostmere Isle holds the **Shrine of the First Flame** (+10% hero XP, +2 hp/s), reached by the Isle Causeway from the north shore.

**Getting there.** **Rimewatch** (the outpost) is on the Highroad. The Scarp Stair comes up from Greyfall.

**Camp.** **Hollowpeak Den** houses brutes and feeds the north-east raid down the Highroad.

**Teaches.** A region whose value is a shrine and fish, not a front.

## Saltmere `saltmere` · tier 3

**What it is.** The harbour and its old quay. The **trading post** here is the only building that turns time into coins. Also here:
- the Saltmere Light shrine (+20% trade, reveals the coast);
- the lamp-keepers (survivors);
- houses and a fishery.

**Camp.** The **Drowned Bell**, round a sunken bell tower in the marsh, raids the harbour and the salt road.

**Crossings.** **Reedwater Ford** is the back door to Barrowmoor.

## Irontooth Foothills `irontooth` · tier 3

**What it is.** Rust hills across the Gorge Bridge. Open-cast iron below the Irontooth, a landmark crag you can see from Greyfall.

**Camp.** The **Irontooth Warcamp** holds the ore and is the east approach's muster. Burning it closes the east approach for good.

**Outpost.** **Rustgate**, the first dry ground over the bridge.

**Connections.** The Seamgate leads down into Deepvein.

## Barrowmoor `barrowmoor` · tier 3

**What it is.** An old battlefield that never finished: sunken barrows, battlefield caches, the Shrine of the Fallen (+10% soldier health), and the Captain's barrow (the Captain's Horn relic).

**Stronghold.** **Gallows Hill** is the west approach's muster, guarded by the **Gallows Knight**.

**Crossings.** The **Bonepass** leads south into Ashgate.

**Outpost.** **Gallowgate**, where three roads meet.

## The Kettle `kettle` · tier 3

**What it is.** Hot springs and yellow sulphur stone. The Kettle Springs shrine makes infirmaries stronger and lets outposts heal.

**Camp.** The **Kettle Warcamp** brews powderkegs (bombers) and is the south-east approach's first muster. It hits Ferrow's east flank.

**The Pass Wall.** This wall (hall 4) caps the Kettle Pass. Once the Kettle camp burns, the south-east comes out of Deepvein through the pass or not at all.

## The Deepwood `deepwood` · tier 3 · optional

**What it is.** The oldest trees: giant timber and the **Heart Oak**.

**Stronghold.** The **Thornmother's Den** feeds the second link of the north-west raid. Killing her gives two relics: the Thorn Crown (hero shots pierce one more enemy) and the Heart-Oak Seed (+25% wood).

**Outpost.** **Heartwood Lodge**.

**Campaign.** Never required.

## Deepvein `deepvein` · tier 4

**What it is.** Deep metal and the first **crystal delve** (the violet pocket), sealed behind the Ironwall and the Slagwall.

**Entrances.** The Seamgate from Irontooth, and the Kettle Pass from the Kettle.

**Stronghold.** The **Seam Overseers** sit on the Haul Road and are the south-east's second muster. Their boss drops the Overseer's Lash (workers +15%).

**Crossings.** **Smelter's Ramp** leads down to Cinderfall.

## The Blackened Rim `rim` · tier 4

**What it is.** The last ground before the Scar. You need the Ferrow Muster burned to claim it.

**The Stair.** The **Cinder Stair** is the south approach's root. The **Stair Wall** (hall 4) and a cannon tower cap its top. With them standing, the south approach breaks on the Rim instead of the Ferrow farms.

**Stronghold.** The **Stairwarden's Bastion** guards the Stair. Its fall opens the road to the Crown and gives the Warden's Aegis (towers +10% range).

## Ashgate `ashgate` · tier 4

**What it is.** The horde's great fortress: a fortress-tier camp of 12000 hp.

**Resources.** Ash crystal fields, obsidian and black iron.

**Outpost.** **Ashfall**, at the foot of the Bonepass.

**Burning Ashgate** drops the fire wall on the Regent's Causeway. The **Bone Maw** remains as the west approach's permanent muster until the Regent dies.

**Connections.** The **Obsidian Bridge** over the Cinderrun is the only way east into the Crown.

## The Cinder Crown `crown` · tier 5

**What it is.** The Regent's caldera: a ring of lava around the island throne. It needs the Stairwarden burned to claim, and hall 5 (Citadel).

**The Ashen Maw** is the south approach's permanent muster.

**The finale.** The **Regent's Causeway** opens when Ashgate burns. Crossing it wakes the Regent.

**Outpost.** **Emberfall**, at the foot of the Stair.

## Cinderfall `cinderfall` · tier 5 · optional

**What it is.** Slag, smoke, and the richest crystal on the frontier.

**Stronghold.** The **Slag Forges** arm the south-east horde. It is optional, and the road to the Crown doesn't pass it.

**The Slag Maw** is the south-east approach's permanent muster.

**Outpost.** **Slagwatch**.
