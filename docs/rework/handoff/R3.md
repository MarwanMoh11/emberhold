# R3 · The march holds its course: handoff

- **Landed (C1–C3).** Front walkers on claimed ground advance (`acquireAdvance`: hero/ally within `HOLD.advanceSight` or 2× just after a close hit, a structure within `HOLD.advanceBump`, else the hall down its field; the wall latch unchanged); assault as before once `inHold`. Raids keep `acquire` with holdings weighted `HOLD.raidHoldings` (0.5). Fronts stagger `FRONT_STAGGER` s apart; the fight timeout stretches by the largest offset. Each route has a `post`.
- **Contracts.** `HOLD = { assaultRadius: 900, advanceSight: 220, advanceBump: 110, raidHoldings: 0.5, provokedSeconds: 2 }` (end of balance.ts) · `RegionManager.inHold(x: number, y: number): boolean` (pure `inHold` exported from `systems/Approaches.ts`) · `Enemy.advancing: boolean` (+ `hitX/hitY/hitT`) · `FRONT_STAGGER = 10`, `POST_BACK = 160`, `frontStagger(plan, id): number`, `frontPost(route: Pt[], hold: (x, y) => boolean, back?): { x: number; y: number } | null` in `Approaches.ts` · `TonightRoute.post: { x: number; y: number }` · `BuildingManager.nearestStructure(x, y, radius, preferDefense = false, holdings = 1)`.
- **Deviations.** `FRONT_STAGGER` lives in `Approaches.ts` (beside `RAID_SHARE`), not balance. A front walker knocked off its march *before* claimed ground also advances (else it could still pick a 700 px farm at the edge). "Whatever last damaged it" is approximated by the hit's source point (projectiles report their impact point, so any close hit widens sight to 440 px for the hero/allies, never for buildings).
- **Sacked.** `nearestStructure` skips `(b as { sacked?: boolean }).sacked`; R4 can drop the cast now that `sacked` is a real field.
- **Gotchas.** The hold region reaches past 900 px on the south and west roads, so posts sit ~1200–1300 px from the hall. Advancing walkers hit a lot of holdings that stand within 110 px of the road (smoke: 878 bump vs 1159 hall target samples) — R4's sacking makes that cheap; R7 may shrink `advanceBump`.
- **Merge.** `WaveManager`: `private stagger` field, `this.stagger = 0` after `splitBudget` in `beginNight` (queue part), `late +` on each spawn `at`, `+ this.stagger` on `timedOut`; `routesFor` builds `post`. `balance.ts`: `HOLD` appended at the end (R1's `TITHE` also appends there).
- **Smoke (wave 8, south + west, ferrow and hollow claimed with 32 holdings):** first spawn south 0.2 s, west 10.5 s; advancing targets hall 1159 / bump 878 / hero 71 / far 0; night ended at 53.5 s. Tests 168 green (new `tests/march.test.mjs`), typecheck clean, lint 0/0.
